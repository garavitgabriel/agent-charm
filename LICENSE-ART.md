## What counts as character art

- Dex and Coach Beard: the frames and sources in `ui/assets-src/dex/` and `ui/assets-src/coach/`
- The sprite data generated from those frames: `ui/charm_assets_sprites.cpp`
- Design renders and character sheets: `docs/design/` and `docs/coach/`
- Screenshots and animations of the characters: `docs/media/`

Fonts under `ui/assets-src/fonts/` keep their own OFL license (see
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)).

## Using your own character

Building a charm for yourself, forking it or experimenting with it is non-commercial use, and
that's fine. If you ship a product, swap in your own character:
[README § Make your own character](README.md#make-your-own-character) shows how.
